Sepsis, unspecified organism. Type 2 diabetes mellitus, well controlled.

who presented with fever and hypotension. Blood cultures were drawn and the
patient was diagnosed with sepsis, unspecified organism, on admission. There
was no evidence of pneumonia on chest imaging.

HOSPITAL COURSE:
The patient was started on broad-spectrum antibiotics and fluid resuscitation.
He improved clinically over the following 72 hours. No evidence of diabetes-
related complications was noted during this admission.

Stable, afebrile, tolerating oral intake.
